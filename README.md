# RustBlox

A free, community-made third-party Roblox launcher for Windows, by ItsNiki66.

## About us

RustBlox is a non-profit community project made by ItsNiki66. It has no ads, no paywalls and no tracking, and it never asks for money.

It exists because playing Roblox should be fast, transparent and in your control: you decide how your client runs, which mods it loads, and what information leaves your computer.

## Features

- Automatic Roblox updates on every launch, so you never fall behind
- Simple installer, no admin rights needed
- Mod packs with a switch for each one; original files are backed up and restored
- FPS cap, including uncapped, and a FastFlag editor
- Activity tracking: your server, its region and your play history
- Discord Rich Presence
- News page where every post stays visible, even ones you missed
- Quality-of-life tools: repair, cache and log cleanup, diagnostics export
- "Did you know?" tips while Roblox downloads

## Download

Get the latest installer from the [Releases](../../releases/latest) page and run `RustBlox-Setup-x.y.z.exe`.

Windows may show a "Windows protected your PC" screen because the installer is new and not yet code-signed. Choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
